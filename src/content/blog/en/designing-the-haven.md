The Haven rents furnished apartments and villas in two cities, Bujumbura and Kigali. When I designed its website, the brief fitted in one sentence: help someone find a verified place and trust the price.

## Show the price first

The site states monthly prices in US dollars, up front. Someone comparing places should not have to send a message just to learn what a month costs. Transparent pricing is the first trust signal, so it sits where people look first.

## Verified, not just listed

Every apartment and villa on the site is verified, and the design makes that visible instead of burying it. For someone booking from another country, "is this real?" is the first question, and the page has to answer it before they ask.

## French first

I wrote the site in French first, because that is the language of the business. Design decisions that look small, like the length of a French label compared with its English one, change how a navigation bar or a button needs to be built.

## Why Astro

Astro ships almost no JavaScript by default. For pages that are mostly photos and text, that means fast loading, and I designed for phones and mobile data first. A rental site that is slow on a phone loses people before they ever see the apartment.

## The lesson

On a rental website, the design decisions are trust decisions: clear prices, proof that places are real, and a language people read comfortably. The technology just has to stay out of the way.
