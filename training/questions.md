# Questions for Bebeto: your answers train the assistant

How to fill this in
- Write your answer under each question, in your own words, as if you were talking to a visitor.
- 1 to 4 sentences is perfect. English or French, whichever feels natural. If you can, answer the important ones in both.
- Only true things. If you don't want to answer, write SKIP.
- Anything you write here may end up in the assistant's answers, so don't put private details (home address, passwords, family matters).
- Replace the line "ANSWER:" with your answer.
